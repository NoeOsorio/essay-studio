# Quickstart: verify the new icon

Prerequisites: the icons are regenerated in the repo and Noe has approved the swap of the installed app.

1. Open the installed Essay Studio.
2. Screenshot the real Dock with its neighbors. Expected: the notes artwork fills the rounded square, no gray plate, same size as its neighbors within 2 pixels.
3. Confirm only the expected copies of the app are running, and that each one shows the new icon.
4. Check Finder, the app switcher (Command Tab) and the About panel. Expected: new artwork in each.
5. Run the web content and look at the tab icon. Expected: new artwork.
6. Open each generated size in `src-tauri/icons`. Expected: no parchment.
7. Open an existing essay. Expected: it opens as before.
8. Give the Dock screenshot to an independent reviewer. Expected: 7 or more on cozy, belongs to the app, fills the shape, reads small and looks professional.
9. Noe confirms in his Dock.
